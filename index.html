About / field note 00

A town built for the situation.

Acid Goblin is an ongoing experiment in making strange, useful little experiences. It uses generated images and a frankly cheerful amount of Python to turn a growing goblin world into zines, newspapers, posters and places to poke around.

Every route is tuned to the goblin preferences and fire circumstances of its creator: what feels good to look at, what needs saying, and which kind of emergency wants a tiny field guide instead of a corporate memo.

No universal solution promised. Just a small town with a colour printer, an overstuffed tool shed, and room to keep changing.
